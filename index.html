Professional profile

Research with purpose.
Teaching with impact.

Economics research that connects rigorous analysis with practical questions of sustainability, energy, finance and wellbeing.

“My work explores how economic choices and policy can support a more sustainable and inclusive future.”

Dr. Nor Salwati Othman is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Accounting and Finance at UNITEN Business School (UBS), and a Research Associate at UNITEN’s Institute of Energy Policy and Research (IEPRe). Her doctoral work examined the dynamic interactions between carbon dioxide emissions and their determinants in the context of sustainable urban development in Malaysia.

Her research brings together environmental and energy economics, economic development, financial economics and business economics. She has contributed to international journal articles, conference proceedings, technical reports and research book chapters, and leads and contributes to funded projects with academic, industry and policy partners.
